# Tossly — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 May 2026
**Last updated:** 11 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Tossly** mobile application (the
"App"), developed by **Pham Gia** ("we", "us"), handles information when
you use the App. By using Tossly you agree to the practices described
below.

We respect your privacy and try to keep data collection to the
minimum needed to keep the App working, stable, and free.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We **do not require an account**, and we do not ask for your name,
email, phone number, address, photos, contacts, or location.

We do collect a limited amount of technical and usage information
through the third-party services listed in Section 2:

| Category | Examples | Source |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Device & app info | Device model, OS version, app version, language, country | Firebase Analytics |
| Usage events | Which mode you flipped, when settings or history are opened | Firebase Analytics |
| Crash diagnostics | Stack traces, OS state at time of crash, anonymous device identifier | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Advertising identifiers | Advertising ID (Android), IDFA (iOS — only if you allow tracking) | Google AdMob |
| Coarse advertising signals | Approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction events | Google AdMob |

We **never** collect:

- Your name, email, or phone number
- Your precise location (no GPS access)
- Photos, contacts, or microphone data
- The contents of any custom labels or list items you type — those are
  stored only on your device and never leave it

All settings, custom labels, custom colors and flip history are kept
**locally on your device** using the operating system's secure storage
(AsyncStorage). They are not synced to any server.

---

## 2. Third-party services

Tossly uses the following services. Each has its own privacy policy
that you should review:

- **Google Firebase Analytics** — anonymous usage analytics so we can
  understand which features are used.
  [Firebase Privacy Information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Firebase Crashlytics** — automatic crash reporting so we can
  fix bugs.
  [Firebase Privacy Information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Firebase Remote Config** — lets us toggle features without a
  new release; collects no personal data.
- **Google AdMob** — shows ads inside the App. AdMob may collect an
  advertising identifier and basic interaction data.
  [Google Ads Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

These services act as our **data processors** under applicable privacy
law (GDPR, CCPA).

---

## 3. Advertising

Tossly displays a banner and an occasional full-screen ad on app open,
served by Google AdMob.

- **On Android**, ads use your device's Advertising ID. You can reset
  this ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time:
  *Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads
  personalization*.
- **On iOS 14.5+**, we ask once for App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
  consent. If you decline, ads are still shown but in
  **non-personalized** form (no IDFA).

You can opt out of personalized advertising from major ad networks at
[youradchoices.com](https://youradchoices.com/) (US) or
[youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/) (EU).

---

## 4. Children's privacy

Tossly is rated **Everyone / 4+**. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided
us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Ads served in the App are configured for general audiences. We do not
use audience targeting that is intended for children.

---

## 5. Data retention

- **Local data** (settings, custom labels, flip history) lives on your
  device for as long as the App is installed. Uninstalling the App
  deletes all of it.
- **Analytics / crash data** is retained by Google according to
  Firebase defaults (typically 2 to 14 months) and then deleted.

---

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA, UK, California, etc.), you may
have rights to:

- Access the data we hold about you
- Have it corrected or deleted
- Opt out of analytics / advertising

Because we do not collect identifiers tied to your identity (no email,
no account), we cannot single out your data in Firebase. The most
effective way to exercise these rights is to **uninstall the App** —
this stops all data collection from your device.

You can also disable analytics and ad personalization in your device
settings:

- **Android**: *Settings → Google → Ads* and *Settings → Apps → Tossly
  → Permissions*
- **iOS**: *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking* and *Settings →
  Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising*

---

## 7. Data transfers

Firebase and AdMob are operated by Google and may transfer data to
servers located in the United States and other countries. Google
maintains [Standard Contractual
Clauses](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks) for EU/EEA
data transfers.
