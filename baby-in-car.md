# Baby-in-Car — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 8 August 2026
**Last updated:** 8 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Baby-in-Car** mobile application
(the "App"), developed by **Pham Gia** ("we", "us"), handles information
when you use the App. By using Baby-in-Car you agree to the practices
described below.

Baby-in-Car exists to remind you that a child is in the car. Doing that
means the App holds details about your family — names, ages, photos,
where you drive. **All of it stays on your device.** We have no server,
no account system, and no way to read any of it.

---

## 1. Information you enter, and where it stays

Everything below is stored **only on your device**, using the operating
system's app storage. It is never uploaded, never synced, and never
visible to us:

| What | Examples |
|------|----------|
| About your children | Name, birth year, boy/girl, avatar colour, and a photo if you add one |
| About you | The name you enter for yourself, and the name of an emergency contact (a name only — no phone number or email) |
| About your car | The Bluetooth name and identifier of the car you pair with |
| Places | Names and addresses you save, such as home or daycare |
| Schedules | Titles, days and times you set for school runs and similar trips |
| Trip history | Start and end times, which children rode along, how many reminders were sent, whether you acknowledged one, and — if you allow location access — the coordinates where each trip started and ended |
| Settings | Alert loudness, repeat counts, which detection methods are on |

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

---

## 2. Device permissions and why the App asks

The App asks only for what the reminder actually needs. Each is optional
in the sense that you can refuse it — but refusing some of them means the
App cannot detect trips, which is its whole purpose.

- **Location (While Using and Always)** — used to detect when a drive has
  started and ended, and to stamp each trip in your history with where it
  began and finished. "Always" is required on iOS so detection keeps
  working when the App is not on screen. Coordinates are written to your
  trip history on your device and are **not** transmitted to us or to
  anyone else.
- **Motion & Fitness / Physical activity** — used to recognise that you
  are driving, as an alternative to Bluetooth detection.
- **Bluetooth** — used to notice when your phone connects to or
  disconnects from your car, which is how the App knows a trip started or
  ended.
- **Notifications** — used to deliver the reminder. This is the point of
  the App; without it there is nothing to alert you.
- **Camera and Photos** — used only if you choose to add a picture to a
  child's profile. The image is saved into the App's own storage on your
  device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

The App does **not** record audio, does not access your contacts, and
does not read your messages, call history, or files.

## 3. Information collected by third-party services

While your family's data stays on your device, the App does include
services from Google that collect limited technical information:

| Category | Examples | Source |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Device & app info | Device model, OS version, app version, language, country | Firebase Analytics |
| Automatic app events | App opened, session length, screen views, app updated | Firebase Analytics |
| Crash diagnostics | Stack traces, app state at the time of a crash, an anonymous device identifier | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Advertising identifiers | Advertising ID (Android), IDFA (iOS — only if you allow tracking) | Google AdMob |
| Coarse advertising signals | Approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction events | Google AdMob |

We do not send any custom events describing your children, your trips,
your places, or your schedules. Analytics reports only the standard app
lifecycle events listed above.

Note that the approximate location AdMob derives from your IP address is
separate from, and much coarser than, the GPS coordinates in your trip
history — those never leave your device.

## 4. Third-party services

Baby-in-Car uses the following services. Each has its own privacy policy
that you should review:

- **Google Firebase Analytics** — anonymous usage analytics so we can
  understand how the App is used.
  [Firebase Privacy Information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

  advertising identifier and basic interaction data.
  [Google Ads Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

These services act as our **data processors** under applicable privacy
law (GDPR, CCPA).

## 5. Advertising

Baby-in-Car is free, and ads are what pays for it. The App shows a banner
above the tab bar and, on some launches, a full-screen ad while it opens.
Ads never appear during setup, and never on the first screen you reach
after finishing setup.

- **On Android**, ads use your device's Advertising ID. You can reset it
  or opt out of personalised ads at any time: *Settings → Google → Ads →
  Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads personalisation*.
- **On iOS 14.5+**, we ask once for App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
  consent. If you decline, ads are still shown but in
  **non-personalised** form (no IDFA), and the App behaves identically in
  every other way.

You can opt out of personalised advertising from major ad networks at
[youradchoices.com](https://youradchoices.com/) (US) or
[youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/) (EU).

## 6. Children's privacy

Baby-in-Car is designed **for parents and carers**, not for children. The
App is not directed to children and is not intended to be used by them.

The App does hold information *about* children — a name, a birth year, a
photo — because that is what a reminder needs in order to be useful. That
information is entered by an adult, stored only on the device, and is
never transmitted to us, to Google, or to any advertiser. We have no
access to it and cannot retrieve it.

Ads served in the App are configured for a general audience. We do not
use audience targeting intended for children.

If you believe a child has used the App and provided information in a way
that concerns you, contact us at the address in Section 10 — although in
practice, deleting the child's profile in the App, or uninstalling the
App, removes the data completely, because there is no other copy.

---

## 7. Data retention

- **Local data** (children, car, places, schedules, trip history,
  settings, photos) lives on your device for as long as the App is
  installed. Deleting a profile removes it immediately; uninstalling the
  App removes everything.
- **Analytics and crash data** is retained by Google according to
  Firebase defaults (typically 2 to 14 months) and then deleted.

---

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA, UK, California, etc.), you may have
rights to access, correct, or delete the data held about you, and to opt
out of analytics and advertising.

For everything you entered into the App, you can exercise those rights
directly: edit or delete any child, place, schedule or trip from within
the App, or uninstall it to remove all of it at once.

For the technical data collected by Firebase and AdMob, we do not collect
identifiers tied to your identity (no email, no account), so we cannot
single out your records. Uninstalling the App stops that collection from
your device. You can also limit it in device settings:

- **Android**: *Settings → Google → Ads* and *Settings → Apps →
  Baby-in-Car → Permissions*
- **iOS**: *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking* and *Settings →
  Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising*

## 9. Data transfers

Firebase and AdMob are operated by Google and may transfer data to
servers located in the United States and other countries. Google
maintains [Standard Contractual
Clauses](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks) for EU/EEA data
transfers.

Data you enter into the App is not transferred anywhere, because it never
leaves your device.
